# About Intertek Group

Intertek Group was founded in Panama in 2006 and combines maritime law, survey work, and compliance services across multiple jurisdictions.

## Focus

- Marine registration and mortgage work
- Survey and technical support
- ISM and ISPS compliance
- Naval architecture and consultancy

## Markets

- Deepsea shipping
- Offshore vessels, tugs, and workboats
- Government and port authorities
- Fishing boats and pleasure yachts
