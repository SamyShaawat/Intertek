# Intertek Group

Marine Registration, Surveys & Compliance.

Founded in Panama in 2006, Intertek Group supports ship registration, marine surveys, ISM/ISPS compliance, and naval architecture across 11 countries.

## What we do

- Ships' registration
- Offshore corporates' registration
- Mortgages registration
- Crew documentation and endorsement
- Class H&M services and consultancy
- Statutory services and consultancy
- Naval architecture and consultancy
- ISM and ISPS services and consultancy
- Third-party marine surveys
- Pre- and post-PSC inspections
- Incident and near-miss response attendance
- MLC crew welfare inspections

## Coverage

- 24/7 global response
- Surveyor presence in 11 countries
- Core offices in Panama City, Mississauga, and Dubai
